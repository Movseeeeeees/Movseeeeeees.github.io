Dead Road Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

In short: Dead Road has no accounts and we do not collect your name, email or contacts. Your progress is saved only on your device. The game is free and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, and those ad partners collect some device information to show and measure ads. You choose whether ads may be personalized, and you can change that at any time in the game's Settings.

1. Who we are

Dead Road ("the game") is made by Movses, an independent game developer ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, write to movsesmardanyan@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone or tablet: levels and stars, coins, upgrades, best endless distance, game settings and your answer to the ads question. This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

3. Advertising

We use Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies and its affiliate ironSource, to show ads. LevelPlay may load ads from the ad networks it works with, currently Unity Ads and ironSource Ads. When an ad is requested or shown, these partners may automatically collect:

  • the advertising identifier of your device (Google Advertising ID on Android; on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking),
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device model, operating system, language, time zone and network type,
  • app information such as the game version, and
  • how you interact with ads (views, clicks, completed reward videos).

They use this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance, detect fraud and, only if you agree, show ads that match your interests. We do not receive or store this information ourselves, except for anonymous, combined reports about ad performance.

How Unity and ironSource handle this data is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices

  • Personalized ads: the first time you open the game we ask whether ads may be personalized. You can change your answer at any time in Settings with the PERSONALIZED ADS switch. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.
  • Device settings: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On iOS you can turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" in Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.
  • Reward videos are optional: videos for extra coins or a second chance only play when you tap a button marked AD.

5. Legal bases and your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, personalized ads are shown only with your consent, and non personalized ads and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interest. You may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent at any time. If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, choosing "No" (generic ads only) tells our ad partners not to sell or share your personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information ourselves.

Because we do not hold personal data about you on our side, requests about data collected by ad partners are best sent to them through the Unity policy linked above. You can also email us and we will help.

6. Children

Dead Road is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The game tells our ad partners that it is not child directed. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and security

Game data on your device is kept until you uninstall the game. Ad partners keep data for the periods described in their own policies. We use the security features of the app stores and of our partners' SDKs, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes, for example if we add new ad partners or features. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
